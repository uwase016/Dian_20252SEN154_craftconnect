
# CraftConnect Rwanda
## Artisanal Products and Order Management System

### 1. Problem Statement
Local artisans may face difficulties managing their handmade products, available stock, customer orders and payment records. Poor record management can make it difficult to monitor sales and organize business activities.

### 2. Proposed Solution
CraftConnect Rwanda will be an Oracle database system that helps artisans manage products, customers, inventory, orders and payments.

### 3. Data Source
Rwanda statistical information and verified research sources about handicrafts and the creative industry will support the project. Additional sample records will be documented where necessary.

### 4. Planned Tables
- ARTISAN
- CUSTOMER
- CATEGORY
- PRODUCT
- ORDERS
- ORDER_ITEMS
- PAYMENT
- STOCK_MOVEMENTS

### 5. Planned PL/SQL Features
- Procedures for registering artisans and placing orders.
- Functions for calculating order totals.
- Cursors for displaying order and sales reports.
- Triggers for automatic stock updates.
- Packages for organizing business operations.
- Audit logs for tracking important changes.

### 6. Innovation
An Oracle APEX application with an interactive dashboard for managing artisans, products, inventory and orders. It will include automated stock updates and low-stock alerts.

### 7. References
Verified academic, statistical, dataset, existing-system and technical sources will be included.
